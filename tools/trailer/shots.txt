# Trailer commands (presentation only, nothing in the game changes):
#   film zoom <level> <seconds>            glide to a zoom level: 0 closest, 2 normal, 4+ far out
#   film camera <yaw> <tilt> <seconds>     glide to a 3D angle: yaw 0..360, tilt 40..180 (100 = classic)
#   film orbit <+yaw> <+tilt> <seconds>    turn the camera at a steady speed
#   film pan <x> <y> <seconds>             glide the view to a tile
#   film follow <vehicle>|off              keep the view centred on a vehicle
#   film ride <vehicle> cab|chase          full-screen ride; film ride off to return
#   film ui off|on                         hide or show toolbars, windows and the mouse cursor

#   renderer_3d on|off    scrollto instant <x> <y>    zoomto <level>    renderer_3d camera <yaw> <tilt>
